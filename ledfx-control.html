Onset Methods (Beat & Transient)

hfcHigh-Frequency Content — sharp percussive hits (hi-hats, snares, claps).
energySignal energy jumps — strong response to kick drums and bass drops.
complexMagnitude + phase change — balanced for mixed melodic & percussive tracks.
specfluxSpectral flux (L2) — smooth transient tracking for electronic & dance music.
specdiffSpectral difference (L1) — general-purpose onset detector.
phasePhase deviation — sensitive to tonal and harmonic note starts.
wphaseWeighted phase — ignores quiet background noise while tracking note starts.
klKullback-Leibler divergence — highlights sudden new frequency energy.
mklModified KL divergence — like kl with smoother handling of quiet frames.

Pitch Methods (Dominant Note)

yinfftSpectral YIN — accurate all-round pitch detection with good noise immunity.
yinTime-domain YIN — very accurate on clean bass/monophonic notes (more CPU).
yinfastFast FFT YIN — lower CPU usage with slightly coarser pitch tracking.
schmittSchmitt trigger — ultra-low CPU; best only for simple, clean waveforms.
specacfSpectral autocorrelation — emphasizes harmonic periodicity in synth/instrument tones.

Thresholds & Timing

  • Pitch Tol (0.0–1.0): Confidence threshold for pitch tracking. Higher values accept looser/more reactive pitch matches; lower values require stricter harmonic clarity.
  • Peak Isol (0.0–0.99): Spectral peak isolation. Emphasizes dominant frequency peaks over background noise across Bass, Mid, and High bands.
  • Delay (0–1000 ms): Delays light output to stay in sync with Bluetooth, soundbar, or DSP speaker latency.
